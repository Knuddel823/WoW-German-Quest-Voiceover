# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — WoW Forever and revised Classic recordings

- Added German voiceovers for WoW Forever quests, including many quests that are new in WoW Forever (about 1,900 recordings).
- Revised a large part of the Classic recordings:
  - Fixed quest texts that contained unrelated content, such as user comments or item lists.
  - Added quest phases that were previously missing, for example quests that start at an object.
  - The player is now consistently addressed as "Held", and gendered forms such as "Bruder/Schwester" are spoken correctly.
  - Improved pronunciation of place names such as Ironforge and Stormwind, and of words written in capital letters.
  - Descriptive passages in quest texts are now read by a narrator voice.
- Removed recordings for quests that are not available in the game.
- 13,929 German recordings in total.

## 0.1.0 — Initial release

- First German quest voiceover release for Chatty Little NPC.
- 12,398 German recordings included.
- German recordings are delivered as a separate `deDE` add-on.
- Quest coverage is incomplete. English fallback can be enabled in Chatty Little NPC for recordings that are not yet available in German.
